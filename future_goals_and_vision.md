## 1. Core Vision Statement

Tushar Singh’s long-term vision centers on **unifying advanced artificial intelligence with hardened, high-throughput distributed backend engineering**. While the AI industry is experiencing an explosion of foundational models, the critical bottleneck remains system-level integration: making models fast, cost-effective, self-correcting, fault-tolerant, and usable by everyday people.

Tushar envisions building autonomous, agentic platforms capable of perceiving their environments (through multimodal vision and spatial data), reasoning over complex statutory or domain-specific knowledge bases (via advanced RAG and graph algorithms), and taking autonomous actions via reliable API microservices.

### Phase 1: Short-Term Targets (Next 6 – 12 Months)

1. **Mastery of Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Workflows**:
   - Deepen expertise in **LangGraph**, **CrewAI**, and **AutoGen** to build self-directed autonomous agent systems featuring reflection loops, tool calling, and dynamic plan-and-solve execution.
   - Implement the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** to connect LLM agents directly with external developer tools, APIs, and databases.
2. **Deep Learning & Model Optimization**:
   - Transition from prompt engineering to parameter-efficient fine-tuning (**PEFT**, **LoRA**, and **QLoRA**) on domain-specific open-weights models (Llama 3, Mistral, Gemma).
   - Implement aggressive edge quantization (**AWQ**, **GGUF**, **INT8 / INT4**) and model compilation with **TensorRT** and **ONNX Runtime** for zero-latency inference.
3. **Distributed Systems Engineering in Go (Golang)**:
   - Augment Python backend mastery by building high-concurrency microservices in **Go**, utilizing goroutines and channels to handle tens of thousands of concurrent WebSocket connections.
   - Integrate distributed streaming brokers (**Apache Kafka**, **Redis Pub/Sub**) for real-time video frame pipelines and event-driven architectures.
4. **Targeted Internship Placement**:
   - Secure a competitive **AI/ML Engineering** or **Backend Software Development Internship** for Summer/Winter, contributing directly to production services in a fast-paced environment.
